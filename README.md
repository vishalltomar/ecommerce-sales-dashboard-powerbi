# E-commerce Economy – 2026 Market Overview (Power BI Dashboard)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes e-commerce sales across cities, product categories and price ranges to understand where revenue comes from.

![Dashboard Preview](dashboard.png)

## Objective
To analyze e-commerce sales data and answer:
- Which cities and categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products are the top sellers?
- How does product price relate to revenue?

## Dashboard Features
- **KPI cards:** Total Revenue (~6M), Total Orders (~1K), Avg Order Value (6.16K), Total Quantity Sold (~2K), Top Product Revenue (838K), Unique Products (9)
- **Revenue by City:** bar chart comparing Chennai, Kolkata, Mumbai, Delhi and Bangalore
- **Revenue by Category:** donut chart for Clothing, Electronics and Groceries
- **Top Selling Products:** table ranked by revenue
- **Price vs Revenue:** analysis of how price affects revenue
- **Interactive slicers:** filter by City and Category

## Key Insights
- Revenue is split almost evenly across categories: Clothing ~34%, Electronics ~33.5%, Groceries ~32%.
- Chennai is the top city by revenue, and Bangalore is the lowest.
- Phone is the top product with ~838K revenue, followed by Bread and Jacket.
